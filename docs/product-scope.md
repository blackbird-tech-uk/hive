# Product Scope

## Version 1

Hive V1 manages Unitech EA660 scanners for Blackbird's event-operations workflow.

Included product areas:

- Device registration and identity
- Device heartbeat and current health
- Event assignment and assignment history
- Pick and return workflows
- Condition, repair, notes, and availability
- Android reporting agent

## Explicitly deferred

Remote commands, alarms, locking, OTA updates, app deployment, Android Enterprise policy control, multi-company tenancy, customer portals, advanced reporting, analytics, and Nora integration are architectural considerations only. They are not Sprint 1 deliverables.
